# Plan: Planetar compute

> First draft, 2026-11-22. Every row is open for discussion in
> [Ideas](https://github.com/SoulCraft-Org/planetar-compute/discussions/categories/ideas).

## Goal

Show how a data center can run on Earth's heat and return power and water to the grid around it.

## Questions to answer

| # | Area | Question | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where | Sites with heat close to the surface, and how deep to drill | ⬜ open |
| 2 | Energy | Heat flow per well, and wells per gigawatt | ⬜ open |
| 3 | Giving back | How much power and water returns to the grid and the town | ⬜ open |
| 4 | Hardware | GPUs per megawatt, and cooling with the plant's own loop | ⬜ open |
| 5 | Cost | Cost per GPU-hour against today's data center | ⬜ open |
| 6 | Pilot | The smallest build beside a working plant | ⬜ open |
